How do I request a quote?

Open the Vendors tab, pick a vendor (or tick several), tap Request a Quote, describe what you need and send. Vendors reply to you directly by email, and you can track every request under My Requests.

How do I list my business?

Create a Vendor account, then tap + List a Vendor and fill in your business details. Quote requests arrive by email and in the Requests to My Vendors tab.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in, open the menu under your name, choose Edit profile, then Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and associated data. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

How do I report a vendor or review?

Tap the Report link on any vendor or review, or email us with the details.
