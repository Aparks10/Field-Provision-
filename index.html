How Field Provisions Works

One place to find trusted vendors for your team and request quotes from several at once — free for coaches.

  1. 1
    Find vendorsSearch, or filter by category, sport and service area. Tap the heart to save favorites.
  2. 2
    Request quotesTap Request a Quote on one vendor, or tick several and send one request to all of them.
  3. 3
    Compare and decideVendors reply to you by email. Track every request under My Requests, then leave a review once you've worked together.
  1. 1
    Create your listingSign up as a vendor, tap + List a Vendor, and add your categories, sports, service area, photo and contact info.
  2. 2
    Receive requestsQuote requests arrive by email and in Requests to My Vendors. Tap “Quote Sent” once you've replied.
  3. 3
    Stand out with ProPro listings appear first in search with a featured badge and can run a promotion banner at the top of the directory.

Common questions

Coaches use Field Provisions free. Vendors can list for free. Optional Pro upgrades are available for extra visibility.

You and the vendor work that out directly. Field Provisions connects you but isn't part of the transaction.

Use the Report link on any vendor or review, or email us below.

Open the menu under your name and choose Edit profile. You can permanently delete your account there.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email, password (encrypted), role (coach or vendor), and school/organization. Vendors additionally provide business details such as phone, email, website, categories, sports served, and photos. When you request a quote or write a review, we store that content along with your profile information.

How We Use It

We use your information to operate the directory, connect Coaches and Vendors, display reviews and listings, send account-related messages (like email confirmation), and — where configured — to notify a Vendor by email when they receive a quote request (including the requesting Coach's name, school/organization, and contact email), and to confirm to the Coach that the Vendor was notified, so the two of you can connect directly.

We also keep simple, anonymous usage counts for vendor listings, such as how many times a listing was shown or opened and how many times its Call, Email or Website link was tapped. These counts are not tied to your name or account, are not used to track you across other apps or websites, and are shown to the listing owner only as totals.

What We Share

Your name, school/organization, and message contents are shared with the specific Vendor(s) you contact, and vice versa, so the two of you can communicate. Vendor listing information (business name, categories, contact info) is public within the app. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) using industry-standard security practices. Photos are stored in secure cloud storage. We retain data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations.

Your Choices

You can edit your vendor listing and remove favorites at any time, and you can permanently delete your account and associated data from Edit Profile in the app (or by emailing us). You may also request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Children's Privacy

Field Provisions is intended for coaches, athletic staff, and vendor businesses — not for use by children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
